iOS 主应用与键盘扩展

  • 允许完全访问：在 iOS“设置 → 通用 → 键盘 → 键盘”中选中宗卡（ཙོང་ཁ），可开启或关闭“允许完全访问”。它用于键盘联网及与主应用共享数据；不开启仍可使用内置藏文键盘、Wylie 转写和本地候选。开启此权限不表示我们会上传普通键入流，也不等于您同意将内容交给第三方 AI。
  • 共享与隔离存储：主应用与扩展使用 App Group 共享设置和词库等数据；未开启完全访问时，键盘个人词库使用扩展私有存储。切换权限后使用的是不同存储位置，词条不会因此自动迁移或合并。访问凭据及账号信息使用共享 Keychain，刷新凭据仅保存在主应用 Keychain。

邮箱验证码通过邮件投递服务发送；网络托管和邮件服务提供方会处理交付服务所需的数据。 在依法必须履行义务或处理安全事件时，我们也可能披露必要信息。

  • 核心邮箱账号及云词典在您使用账号期间保存；您可主动删除云词典或注销账号。
  • 登录验证码通常约 10 分钟有效，访问凭据通常约 15 分钟有效，刷新会话最长通常约 30 天。有效期不等于所有验证或安全记录的自动删除期限。
  • 已同意的主应用日活记录按约 35 天滚动窗口清理；安装注册记录用于统计授权管理，并可在撤回统计授权后删除。
  • 注销时会删除核心账号、账号关联的云词典、验证记录及可关联到该账号的使用统计，并撤销会话。为防止备份恢复已删除账号和处理重复删除请求，单独保留删除标记，包含账号标识、邮箱及其哈希、删除时间和请求编号；该安全记录不随账号一起删除，当前不设置自动到期。
  • 未经账号关联的安装统计可通过应用内统计开关单独撤回；网络安全、防滥用、支持请求及外部处理服务所必需的请求元数据可能依据各自的必要用途继续保留，但不包括本次提交的文字、图片、音频及其处理结果。您可以联系我们查询有关您数据的保留情况或提出删除请求。

Local typing and optional services

Ordinary typing and suggestions are processed locally. We do not upload a continuous stream of keystrokes or ordinary text typed in other apps. Basic keyboard input does not require an account. Some online features require sign-in.

When you choose to use an online feature, we process the data needed for that feature: email addresses and account identifiers for authentication; personal dictionary files for cloud sync; query text for knowledge lookup; submitted text for translation, correction or polishing; selected photos for OCR; and recorded audio for speech recognition. Support requests include information you choose to send to our contact email.

Optional host-app statistics require separate consent and include random installation identifiers and hashes, platform, app version and active dates. Statistics may be associated with an account when signed in. They contain no typed text and are not used for advertising tracking. You can withdraw consent in Settings. Network requests also convey IP addresses and request metadata needed for communications, service operation and abuse prevention. This version has no advertisements or advertising ID collection and does not request location, contacts or SMS permissions.

Permissions, providers and security

Microphone access is used for user-initiated voice input. Camera or selected-image access is used for user-initiated OCR. You can refuse or revoke these permissions. Local files, including audio and image processing files, may remain on your device until removed or app data is cleared.

Our backend provides account, cloud dictionary, knowledge and statistics services. User-initiated AI, OCR and speech features send the corresponding text, photos or audio to processing services, including services provided or connected by Pandita (pandita.com.cn). AI requests may be forwarded through our backend; photos or audio may be sent directly to their processing services. Email delivery and hosting providers process the information needed to operate their services. For text, selected photos, audio, and corresponding results submitted for AI, OCR, or speech processing, Zongkha and the providers performing that request use the content transiently only as needed to complete the request and do not retain it after processing completes. This non-retention statement covers only those request contents and results, not accounts, cloud dictionaries, verification or security records, network request metadata, or local files; those are handled under the relevant sections of this policy. Necessary IP addresses, request times, status, and security metadata may be retained for service operation and abuse prevention, but do not include the submitted content. We make no unconditional statement about whether external providers use request content for model training; that depends on the contract and service rules applicable to the specific service. Contact us about provider handling or deletion requests.

iOS app and keyboard extension

In iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards, select 宗卡 / ཙོང་ཁ to manage Allow Full Access. It enables keyboard networking and shared app data. Built-in Tibetan typing, Wylie transliteration and local suggestions remain available without it. Full Access does not authorize uploading ordinary typing or replace permission to share content with third-party AI services.

App Group storage coordinates settings and dictionaries. Without Full Access the keyboard uses extension-private dictionary storage; changing the permission switches storage locations without automatically migrating or merging entries. Access credentials and account information use shared Keychain; refresh credentials are kept in the companion app only.

Manually open the companion app from your Home Screen for voice input or OCR; the keyboard does not launch it. Voice recording is limited to approximately 20 seconds per recording and submitted for recognition. Confirming an image crop triggers OCR upload of that selected, cropped image, not your entire photo library.

The local recording.wav file may remain after recognition and be overwritten by a later recording. Delete App, rather than Offload App, removes container data. Keychain items are not guaranteed to be removed by uninstalling; the current app clears old sessions when it detects a new installation. Uninstalling does not delete the server account or necessary service metadata.

Account, cloud dictionary, statistics, AI text, image and speech endpoints use HTTPS. Account credentials use encrypted storage on Android and Keychain on iOS. The iOS companion app and keyboard coordinate sessions, settings and feature data through shared Keychain and App Group storage. Online keyboard features may require Allow Full Access, which can be refused or revoked in iOS Settings; this permission does not replace consent to sharing content. Verification codes and refresh credentials are checked using server-side digests. We apply verification limits, expiry and session revocation.

Retention and deletion

Account data and cloud dictionaries are retained while you use the account. Verification codes are normally valid for about 10 minutes, access credentials for about 15 minutes, and refresh sessions for up to about 30 days; validity does not imply automatic deletion of every verification or security record. Host activity records are purged on an approximately 35-day rolling basis. Installation registrations can be deleted when statistics consent is withdrawn.

Deleting an account removes its core account record, cloud dictionary, verification records and account-linked usage statistics, and revokes sessions. A separate deletion marker retains the account identifier, email and its hash, deletion date and request identifier to prevent backup restoration and support repeat deletion requests. This security record currently has no automatic expiry. Statistics not linked to an account can be withdrawn separately using the statistics setting. Necessary security, abuse-prevention, support and external-provider request metadata may remain according to their purposes, but this does not include submitted AI, OCR or speech content or the corresponding results, which are not retained after processing completes.

You can delete your account in the app or through the 宗卡ཙོང་ཁ account and data deletion page after email verification. To request access, correction or deletion of particular data without deleting the account, or to ask about retained records, contact hang1915581435@gmail.com. We verify requesters' identities before handling requests.

iOS usage statistics are disabled until consent is granted and cover only companion-app activity, not keyboard use or typed content. Refusal does not prevent basic input. Withdraw consent under Other Settings → Usage Statistics; pending offline withdrawals retry when the companion app becomes active with connectivity.

Cloud dictionary backup requires login and separate permission. You can back up, restore, delete the cloud copy or disable backup. Disabling backup does not automatically delete an existing cloud copy. The iOS account-deletion flow lets you choose whether to clear the local personal dictionary; other local processing files are not guaranteed to be removed by account deletion. You can also revoke microphone, camera, photo access and keyboard Full Access in iOS Settings.
